# Cached USDT Home balance

This manual recipe requires a disposable wallet with an existing USDT display snapshot and cached exchange rates, plus control over its RPC and exchange-rate connectivity. Network fault injection and aged cache preparation are not provided by the standard journey environment. No payment or funding is required: an explicitly prepared display snapshot is not an on-chain balance and must be identified as such in the test report.

1. Record the wallet address, network, USDT amount, BTC/USD rate and Bitcoin balances in the prepared snapshot. Keep the rate more than ten minutes old and the snapshot isolated from other wallets.
2. With refresh endpoints unavailable, cold-launch the app and open Home. The headline must include the last known USDT conversion, the USDT tile must show the cached token amount, and `TotalBalanceUpdating` must be visible as a loader at the right of the headline row, in the eye control's slot. Nothing below the headline may move when it appears or clears. With a positive cached balance and no activity, the empty-wallet funding prompt must stay hidden. A wallet without a usable snapshot must keep the total unknown instead of silently omitting USDT.
3. Hide balances using the eye control. Both headline and USDT tile must remain hidden. While they are hidden, the eye control keeps its place and `TotalBalanceUpdating` must not show, even if the balance is still updating. Show them again and confirm the cached amounts are unchanged.
4. Restore the owned fixture endpoints with a different current balance/rate. The displayed total must update and the updating indicator must disappear once both are current. Keep the balance and rate responses successful while making the history endpoint fail; the updating indicator must still clear after refresh finishes. Repeat with a failed balance read; the previous amount must remain marked as updating. Repeat with a fresh zero balance; the headline must show the Bitcoin total even without a USDT conversion rate, and an otherwise empty wallet must show its funding prompt.
5. In a separate disposable install using different wallet credentials or network endpoints, the first wallet's snapshot must not appear. Do not reset an existing wallet to perform this check.
6. With only an expired rate available, open an unfunded payment amount flow and attempt conversion/review without submitting. The display cache must not authorize a quote, conversion or payment that requires a fresh rate.

Record the app revision, device, snapshot provenance and each observed result. A seeded display snapshot proves rendering and restart behavior; it does not prove a funded transfer, provider operation or settlement.
